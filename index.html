About

Hi! I am Donghyun Kim, an AI researcher open to postdoctoral and research scientist opportunities. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Yonsei University, advised by Prof. Kyong-Ho Lee. During my Ph.D., I explored how to bridge the gap between knowledge graphs and real-world facts, with a focus on knowledge validation and refinement. Here is my CV.

My research focuses on Knowledge-Integrated Agentic AI, with the goal of building AI systems that can effectively acquire, organize, retrieve, and use knowledge. I am particularly interested in how structured and unstructured knowledge can be integrated into AI systems in an efficient and reliable manner. More recently, my research has expanded toward AI agents that can maintain and adapt evolving knowledge and memory over time, ultimately enabling reliable long-horizon reasoning and action.
